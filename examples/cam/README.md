# CAM examples

## rotary-shop-tools.json

A 129-tool library in SolidCraft's own tool format (the `tools` list of a design's
`manufacture.tools`, with ids left out so they are given on adding). Each entry can be passed
to `cam.tool.add`.

Where it comes from (no manufacturer catalogue data):

- **10 shop tools**: the contributor's own cutters, measured and typed in by hand
  (T1 50 mm facing tool, T4 6.35 mm and T5 3.175 mm end mills, T6/T7 ball end mills,
  T22 and T25-T28 4-8 mm carbide drills).
- **119 placeholder tools**: generic standard sizes generated by a script, not taken from any
  catalogue: flat, ball and drill sizes from 2 to 12 mm and 3/32 to 15/32 in, 90 and 60 degree
  chamfer mills, and 30/60/90 degree V-bits (entered as chamfer tools). Placeholders whose
  numbers clashed with the shop tools were left out, as were T-slot and dovetail cutters (no
  tool kind for them yet).

Feeds are starting values: end mills and chamfer mills at 16000 rpm, drills at 9000 rpm, with
cutting 1000 / plunge 300 mm/min. Tune them for your machine and material.
